02Send plain text first

The most important decision on this page.

Do not open with the designed HTML email

A branded, image-heavy email to a shop owner who has never heard of you reads as a mass mailing — it lands in Promotions, and it invites a delete rather than a reply. Cold wholesale outreach performs better as a short, plainly written note from a person. Save the designed Buyer Pitch for the second email, once someone has replied and actually wants to see the range.

04How to send HTML email outside Marsello

For when you do want the designed version — usually the follow-up.

Gmail

Gmail has no "paste HTML source" box, so you paste the rendered page instead:

  • Open the template file in Chrome or Safari (double-click it)
  • Click on the page, then Cmd + A to select all, Cmd + C to copy
  • In Gmail, hit Compose and Cmd + V. The layout, colours and images carry over because the styling is inline.
  • Send yourself a test first. Gmail occasionally drops background colours on outer table cells.

Zoho Mail (hello@essanceskincare.com)

Same copy-paste method works. Zoho also has a plain-text/HTML toggle in the compose toolbar on desktop, which handles pasted markup more faithfully than Gmail does. Since your domain now authenticates through Zoho for regular mail, sending 1:1 from Zoho is the better choice for outreach.

If you want to personalise all 16 at once

A mail-merge tool sits on top of Gmail and pulls names from a Google Sheet, so each email sends individually with the right first name and shop name:

  • Mailmeteor or YAMM — simplest, free tiers cover 16 sends easily
  • GMass — more powerful, better for follow-up sequences

All three send from your real mailbox, so replies land in your inbox and it still looks like a personal email — which is the whole point.

Two things that will hurt you if ignored

Never put 16 shops in one BCC. Send individually. A visible group send is an instant delete, and bulk-looking mail from a newly authenticated domain is exactly what spam filters are watching for.

Pace it. Your domain authentication is new. Send 3–5 a day over a week rather than 16 in an hour — a sudden burst from a cold domain is the fastest way to land in spam and undo the setup you just finished.

05Working the list

  • Start with the four skincare shops. They already sell what you make, so they need the least convincing and will tell you fastest whether the pitch works.
  • Walk in where you can. Nine of these are in Portland. For a handmade local brand, showing up with samples beats any email — use email only to book the visit.
  • Say one true thing about their shop. Look at their site or Instagram for sixty seconds first. That line is the difference between a reply and a delete.
  • Follow up once, after about a week. Two sentences. Most yeses come on the second touch, and more than two follow-ups on a cold pitch reads as pressure.
  • Track it. Date sent, replied, samples out, ordered. Sixteen is small enough for a spreadsheet and too many to hold in your head.
