Essance on Faire

Broadcast emails (Welcome series, Campaigns) paste into a Marsello Custom-HTML block - hit "Copy HTML to paste", set the subject shown on the card, and keep the Unsubscribe link intact. 1:1 outreach (Wholesale, Celebrity, Press) - open the file in a browser, select all, copy, and paste into a Gmail compose window, then fill the {PLACEHOLDERS}.

All templates are pure ASCII so nothing turns into mojibake when pasted. Companion assets on the Desktop: Essance-Line-Sheet-2026.pdf and Essance-Media-Kit-2026.pdf.
